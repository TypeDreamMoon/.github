# Code of Conduct

This applies to every Dream-series repository under TypeDreamMoon: issues, pull requests,
discussions and reviews.

## What we expect

- Talk about the work, not the person. Criticize code, designs and docs as hard as they deserve;
  leave the author out of it.
- Assume the other side is acting in good faith until they show otherwise.
- Write so that a non-native reader can follow. English and 中文 are both welcome; nobody is
  obliged to answer in a language they do not use.
- Bring evidence. A reproduction, a log line or a screenshot moves a thread further than an opinion.
- Accept a "no". The maintainer decides what goes into the plugins and will say why.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, harassment, or attacks on someone's identity, background or skill level.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Spam, off-topic promotion, or flooding the tracker with unverified machine-generated reports or
  pull requests.
- Sexual or violent content.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, close or lock threads, and block accounts that
keep breaking these rules. There is no fixed ladder of warnings; the response matches the behavior.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment or profile, or reach the maintainer through the
contact shown on the [TypeDreamMoon](https://github.com/TypeDreamMoon) profile. Reports are kept
private.

# 行为准则

适用于 TypeDreamMoon 名下所有 Dream 系列仓库的 issue、PR、讨论区和代码评审。

## 我们期望的

- 对事不对人。代码、设计、文档该怎么批评就怎么批评,别扯到作者身上。
- 默认对方是善意的,除非对方用行动证明不是。
- 写得让非母语读者也能看懂。English 和中文都欢迎;没有人有义务用自己不用的语言回复。
- 拿证据说话。一份复现、一行日志、一张截图,比一句看法更能推动讨论。
- 接受被拒绝。什么进插件由维护者决定,并且会说明理由。

## 不能接受的

- 辱骂、骚扰,或针对他人身份、背景、水平的攻击。
- 公开他人的隐私信息。
- 刷屏、无关推广,或用未经验证的机器生成报告和 PR 淹没仓库。
- 色情或暴力内容。

## 处理方式

维护者可以编辑、隐藏、删除评论,关闭或锁定讨论,并封禁屡次违反的账号。没有固定的警告阶梯,
处理力度与行为相称。

## 举报

使用 GitHub 评论或主页上的 **Report content**,或通过
[TypeDreamMoon](https://github.com/TypeDreamMoon) 主页上的联系方式联系维护者。举报内容保密。
